**TigerAI**, the TigerAI logo (the orange tiger glyph in `assets/brand.png`),
and product names such as *TigerAI Advanced RAG*, *TigerAI Community Edition*,
*TigerAI WebApp* and *TigerAI Catalog* are trademarks of **TigerAI Taiwan**.

The [Apache-2.0 License](LICENSE) covers the source code in this repository.
**It does not grant any rights to the TigerAI name, logo, or product names.**
This is the same model used by Kubernetes, Docker, Grafana, PostgreSQL, and
most permissively-licensed projects.

We want you to use, fork and build on TigerAI freely. The only purpose of this
policy is to prevent customer confusion about what is and isn't an official
TigerAI product.

---

## ✅ Allowed without asking

- Use the unmodified **TigerAI name and logo** to refer to this project — e.g. "Built on TigerAI", "Powered by TigerAI", "Fork of TigerAI for X"
- Distribute unmodified copies of this repo with the original name and logo intact
- Use the name in academic papers, blog posts, talks, tutorials, conference slides
- **Internal business use of any kind** (private fork, internal modifications, custom deployments) — no branding restrictions on what stays inside your company

## ⚠️ Please rebrand if you

- **Fork and substantively modify the codebase** then redistribute or host it as a service publicly. Pick a different name and logo so users know it's not the official TigerAI. A name like "MyCompany RAG (fork of TigerAI)" is fine and explicitly welcomed.
- **Offer it as a paid product or commercial SaaS.** Examples that need rebranding: *TigerAI Plus*, *TigerAI Pro*, *TigerAI Ultimate*, *TigerAI Cloud*, *TigerAI by [your company]*, *TigerAI Hosted*. These names imply an official product tier and confuse the market.
- **Sell support, consulting or hosting using TigerAI in your product/service name.** It's fine to advertise "we support TigerAI deployments" or "we host TigerAI for you" — it's NOT fine to call your offering "TigerAI Support Services".

## ❌ Not allowed (without prior written permission)

- Pretending your fork or product **is** the official TigerAI
- Using a logo that's a derivative or look-alike of the TigerAI tiger glyph in a way that suggests endorsement or affiliation
- Domain names that imply official affiliation — e.g. `tigerai-official.com`, `get-tigerai.com`, `tigerai-enterprise.com`
- Trademark registration of "TigerAI" or any confusingly similar mark in any jurisdiction

## Why this exists

We open-source the Community edition so anyone can self-host RAG knowledge bases
freely. We sell the Pro/Enterprise tiers (on-prem AI, reranking, multimodal
catalog, knowledge graph, scheduling queue) under the TigerAI brand. If anyone
can call their fork "TigerAI Enterprise", customers lose the ability to
distinguish what's the official paid tier — that hurts both us and them.

We're not trying to discourage forks, contributions or commercial uses. We just
need the brand to mean one specific thing.

## Questions?

If you're not sure whether your use is OK — especially borderline cases —
open an issue in this repository or contact us. We'd much rather clarify
upfront than have to ask you to change something later.
